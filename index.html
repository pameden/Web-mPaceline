1 · During the class

See the class before it happens

When your Peloton class starts, mPaceline Pulse detects it and loads the full class plan. Your live metrics are drawn right on top of it, so you always know where you are and what’s next.

  • Upcoming efforts. Graphs show the cadence, resistance and power changes ahead, so you can pace a hard block before it starts.
  • Power Zone cues. Your current zone, the next zone and a countdown to the change, plus an accuracy score for how well you’re holding it.
  • Every discipline. Cadence and resistance on the bike, speed and incline on the tread, stroke rate on the rower.
  • Heart rate. From a chest strap, armband or your Apple Watch.

2 · Over weeks, months and years

Proof your training is paying off

Connect your Peloton account and your entire workout history comes with you. Riding third-party equipment? mPaceline Pulse records every class as you go. Either way, your progress is easy to see.

  • Get measurably stronger. Watch your output, cadence and resistance climb over time.
  • Track your FTP. See every FTP test in one place and your Power Zones move up with it.
  • Compare repeat classes. Took the same ride twice? Put them side by side and see exactly where you improved.
  • Personal records. PR Explorer shows your best output for every class length.
  • Fitness, fatigue and form. Training metrics show when you’re ready to push and when to recover.
  • Stay consistent. Weekly streaks, all-time totals and calendars keep you showing up.

Questions before you download

Do I need a Peloton membership?

To live-track classes and download your history, yes. mPaceline Pulse connects to your Peloton account to get class plans, instructor cues and your workouts. It works with an All-Access membership on Peloton hardware or the Peloton App on your own equipment.

Will it work with my bike, treadmill or rower?

Peloton Bike, Bike+, Tread and Row stream metrics through your Peloton account. For other equipment, mPaceline Pulse connects to Bluetooth-enabled bikes, trainers, treadmills and rowers, with direct support for Keiser M Series bikes. Heart rate can come from a Bluetooth strap or your Apple Watch. Use the free trial to confirm your equipment connects. How connecting works

What device do I run it on?

iPhone, iPad or both, with an optional Apple Watch app for heart rate. Most Peloton hardware owners mount an iPhone. Peloton App users often play the class on an iPad or TV and run mPaceline Pulse on an iPhone, or run both apps together on one iPad. Compare setups

I do Power Zone training. What do I get?

Enter your FTP and mPaceline Pulse builds your zones, shows the cued zone and the next one with a countdown, scores how accurately you hold each zone, and tracks your FTP over time. Power Zones and FTP
