Legacy mPaceline App

If you came here looking for the legacy mPaceline App, we have some disappointing news. This app has been discontinued as of December 2025.

About a year ago, Peloton announced that they would no longer support the legacy method third-party apps used to connect to their services. They provided developers with a new authentication process, along with a developer agreement and a set of criteria that all apps must meet going forward. All of these requirements were reasonable, and Peloton has been extremely supportive and accommodating throughout the transition.

The challenge for us was that the original mPaceline relied on several capabilities that are no longer permitted under the new guidelines. Removing and reworking those features would have required a substantial rebuild. After evaluating the amount of work involved, we chose to simplify the original mPaceline and start fresh with a modern, streamlined version that fully complies with Peloton’s new standards.

The result is mPaceline Pulse, now available in the App Store. It includes most—but not all—of the functionality from the original app, along with a cleaner design and improved reliability. You can download it today and start a one-week free trial to see if it meets your needs.

We recognize that starting over comes with drawbacks. Because the two apps are technically separate, we are not able to migrate user data or carry over existing subscriptions. Developers do not have access to App Store account information, so there is no mechanism for us to verify prior purchase history. We know this may be inconvenient for some users, but at this time the only path forward is to begin a new subscription in mPaceline Pulse.

I apologize for the inconvenience this may cause you.
