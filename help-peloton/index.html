Connect Peloton Account

Adding your Peloton Account

Connecting your Peloton account is what enables mPaceline Pulse to pull class data in real time while you work out. This is how the app knows:

  • Which class you’re taking (bike, tread, row, strength, etc.)

  • Instructor, class type, duration, and other session details

  • Instructor cues and targets (Power Zone callouts, cadence ranges, pace/speed guidance, stroke-rate targets, etc.)

Once connected, mPaceline Pulse can display those cues visually during the class and show how your live equipment metrics (cadence, power, pace, resistance, stroke rate, heart rate, etc.) compare to what the instructor is asking for.

If your Peloton account is not connected, you can still use mPaceline Pulse for tracking, but you may miss key features like:

  • Automatic class detection/details

  • Instructor cue overlays and zone guidance

  • Better context for your workout results (who/what/when)

How to connect your Peloton account

  1. Open mPaceline Pulse

  2. Go to Settings

  3. Tap Peloton Account

  4. Tap Connect Account

  5. Sign in with your Peloton credentials and approve access

How you’ll know it worked

After connecting, you’ll see your Peloton rider name (and often your profile image) displayed in the Peloton Account section of Settings instead of “Connect Account”.
